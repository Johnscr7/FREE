06 / hype detector

🚨 AI Money Reality Check

AI can speed up work. It cannot replace a customer problem, distribution, judgment, or accountability.

“One prompt can make you rich.”
Prompts can assist production; buyers still pay for useful results, quality control, and trust.
“AI trading bots guarantee profit.”
No model guarantees returns. Treat guaranteed-profit claims as a serious warning sign.
“Fully automated YouTube income.”
Content still needs original value, editorial judgment, rights awareness, and an audience.
“Start an AI agency with zero skills.”
Agencies sell implementation and outcomes. Learn enough to reliably deliver before selling.
How we rate opportunities

Reality is not ease.

  • Difficult but legitimate: AI micro-SaaS can require deep skill and time, yet solve a real paid problem.
  • Easy but low-income: Prompt packs can be quick to ship but often face weak differentiation and modest demand.
  • Saturated but profitable: Writing or design services can still work when you have a clear niche and portfolio.
  • Unsaturated but unproven: Multi-agent systems may be promising, but customer demand is still forming.
  • Reality Score considers: existing demand, willingness to pay, technical feasibility, competition, platform dependence, and startup requirements.
Illustrative ratings — not financial advice. Earnings vary dramatically. Revenue ≠ profit ≠ personal income, and no income level is guaranteed.
